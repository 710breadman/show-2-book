## Editorial reasoning that must carry forward

- Read the entire embedded subtitle file and inspect the whole visual sequence before drafting.
- Write a thesis, emotional hinge, subtlety note, and final-message candidates first. The last page should feel earned by the episode, not appended as a generic moral.
- Keep spoken wording as close to embedded subtitles as possible. Every retained quote is audited. Use natural contractions unless the subtitle intentionally uses a full form; document those in `intentional_full_forms`.
- Attribute dialogue frequently and clearly: “said Bluey,” “asked Bingo,” “Mum whispered,” and similar. Narration should be vivid but concise.
- Match each image to the exact adjoining action or emotion. A visually attractive but mismatched frame is a failure.
- Sound effects belong on the image and must sit with the character or object producing them. Several pages were deliberately rerendered to correct this.
- Use two-panel sequences only when they clarify progression. Apply `panel_centering` so important characters are not lost in the half-width crop.
- Preserve understatement. Do not flatten complicated episodes into lectures. The final message may name the larger truth, but the story itself should let looks, pauses, and actions carry meaning.
- Use playful typography and role colors, light page borders, high-resolution shared assets, and restrained dynamic words. The result should feel consistent enough to be a series but specific enough to belong to its episode.

## Standard per-episode workflow

1. Read `SOURCE_DOSSIER.md` and the complete SRT.
2. Inspect all scene and timeline contact sheets at original detail.
3. Author `project/episodes/<slug>/book_spec.json` with cover, story pages, back/message page, research thesis, care note, role colors, exact frames/timestamps, alt text, and optional sound effects.
4. Run:

   `python tools/audit_episode_spec.py <spec> <srt> --out <editorial_audit.json>`

5. Resolve every manual dialogue review, contraction flag, missing alt, and missing frame. Prefer exact subtitle containment and a maximum page density near 45 words; exceed only deliberately.
6. Build:

   `python tools/build_episode_storybook.py <spec> output`

7. Render the PDF to JPEG for visual review, create contact sheets, and inspect every physical page. Open difficult pages individually at original detail.
8. Correct frame/action mismatches, cropping, tiny text, repetitive panels, and misplaced sound effects. Rebuild and rerender.
9. Render the verified PDF to PNG and run `tools/final_qa.py` with the episode's exact page count.
10. Run the document `a11y_audit.py` and `images_audit.py` scripts.
11. Update `project/PRODUCTION_STATUS.md` only after all gates pass.
