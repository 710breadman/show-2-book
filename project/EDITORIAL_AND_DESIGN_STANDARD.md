# Editorial and Design Standard

## Dialogue fidelity

- Keep the subtitle wording unless a line is cut for pacing.
- Preserve contractions, idioms, names, and character-specific grammar.
- Do not convert quoted speech into formal English.
- Attribute a speaker at the first line of a page and whenever the speaker could be ambiguous.
- Narration may describe gesture, expression, or movement visible in the selected frame.
- Every quote in the manuscript must be traceable to a subtitle timestamp.

## Narrative restraint

- State what happens; imply why it matters.
- Put the emotional hinge in the characters' own words whenever possible.
- Do not explain visual jokes.
- Do not add an adult interpretation to a child's line inside the story.
- Reserve the final page for the distilled takeaway.
- For ambiguous or adult-facing subtext, offer emotional truth without a clinical explanation. In *Onesies*, preserve Brandy's longing and the sisters' reconnection; do not turn the children's edition into an explicit fertility lesson.

## Frame selection

- Image and text must refer to the same action window.
- Use the exact visible action when narration names it (plucking, handing, hiding, blinking, striking).
- Prefer frames with open eyes, readable silhouettes, uncluttered gestures, and both participants visible.
- Avoid transitional mouth shapes, half-blinks, motion blur, and frames whose apparent action contradicts the text.
- For a handoff, both giver and receiver should be visible and emotionally readable.
- Effects must sit nearest the source: DING near the striker/targeted character; BLUB near Dad's mouth/water spray.
- If the sequence matters more than one pose, use a clean two-panel image with a narrow separator.

## Typography and color

- Narration: Trebuchet MS or an equivalent humanist rounded sans, 16.5-18 pt in landscape Letter.
- Dialogue: Arial Rounded MT Bold or a comparable rounded display face, used selectively.
- Minimum text contrast: dark navy on pale panels.
- Character colors: Dad blue, Bluey cyan, Bingo orange, Mum magenta, group dialogue violet.
- Sound effects: warm coral/red with a light outline and subtle shadow.
- Avoid all-caps body paragraphs; reserve caps for short effects and the cover title.

## Image preparation

- Extract from the original video at the locked timestamp.
- Preserve 16:9 unless intentionally creating a panel sequence.
- Enlarge to at least 2560 x 1440 for the Letter landscape reading edition using Lanczos resampling.
- Apply only gentle sharpening. Do not denoise so aggressively that line art develops halos.
- Save JPEG at high quality with 4:4:4 chroma or use PNG where file size is acceptable.
- Do not use generative upscaling that redraws faces, paws, props, or expressions.

## Page-level acceptance checklist

- [ ] Dialogue matches subtitles and uses natural contractions.
- [ ] Speaker names are clear.
- [ ] Narration describes the selected frame accurately.
- [ ] No line sounds like invented episode dialogue.
- [ ] No text or effect covers a face or essential prop.
- [ ] Effect placement matches the source of the sound.
- [ ] Text fits without shrinking below the series minimum.
- [ ] Page turn advances action or emotion.
- [ ] The page remains readable at arm's length.

## Book-level acceptance checklist

- [ ] Cover, story, and final page use one coherent visual system.
- [ ] Word count and read-aloud time match the episode's weight.
- [ ] The emotional hinge is present.
- [ ] Community-favourite moments are represented when they support the story.
- [ ] Understated material remains understated.
- [ ] The final message is short, episode-specific, and not preachy.
- [ ] Every PDF page has been rendered and visually inspected.
- [ ] All fonts are embedded and all images have alt text in the editable edition.
